About Me

My name is Aishah Cholmondeley!

My career has been a winding journey so far. I graduated from McGill University in 2019 with a B.Ed in Teaching ESL, found my way to an educational technology company called Studyo, and there began an interest in how millions of useful tools and websites are brought to life every day.

Today, I work as a Business Analyst at a legal tech company called Estateably. In many ways, this role feels fundamentally similar to teaching: both an educator and a business analyst are constantly looking for ways to close gaps. As a teacher, those gaps may be in your student's knowledge, in your lesson, or in the level of interest the students may bring to whatever is planned. Instead of dealing with a lesson and students, a business analyst closes gaps between the product, process, and adoption levels for working professionals.

I believe in both roles, a considered plan and elegant materials are crucial to the objective. Good BAs and teachers alike structure sessions that will appeal to their audience, impart a clear understanding of the material, progress toward a learning or project objective, and reach everyone in their audience (regardless of who may be in the room).

Delivering a lesson or product is important, but it's a static point in time. I am particularly fascinated by the lead-up: the ways in which we plan, design, build, and scaffold both users and students into success.

Outside of my work, I enjoy going to concerts, doing the New York Times' puzzles, and spending time with my wife and cat.
